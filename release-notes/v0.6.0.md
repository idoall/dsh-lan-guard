<h3 id="cn-v0.6.0">📱 手机上按住刻度，就能看到那一轮在讲什么</h3>

兼容 DeepSeek Harness `0.2.1-alpha.1`。

- 手机上轻点刻度只会跳转，看不到内容——现在按住约 0.3 秒会弹出卡片，显示这一轮的用户提问和回复摘要。
- 卡片上有 ◀ ▶ 逐轮翻看，还有「跳到第 N 轮」，不用再瞄准很细的刻度。
- 轻点跳转、拖动滚动都和以前一样；轨道往内挪了一点、刻度加粗，更好按。

<h3>升级</h3>

```sh
dsh plugin --profile web add dsh-lan-guard@latest
```

重启 dsh 一次。

<h3 id="en-v0.6.0">📱 Hold a tick on your phone to see what that turn said</h3>

Compatible with DeepSeek Harness `0.2.1-alpha.1`.

- On a phone a tap only jumped, so the preview was unreachable; holding a tick for about 0.3s now opens a card with that turn's prompt and reply.
- The card has ◀ ▶ to step through turns and a "jump to turn N" button, so you no longer aim at a hair-thin tick.
- Tapping and dragging still work as before; the rail moved slightly inward and its ticks are thicker.

<h3>Upgrade</h3>

```sh
dsh plugin --profile web add dsh-lan-guard@latest
```

Restart dsh once.
